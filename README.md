## ⟡ Who I Am

I'm **Zein Al-Ali**. I'm not learning security theory in a vacuum — I'm sitting inside a live SOC queue, triaging real alerts, and writing down exactly what I find, wrong turns included.

I don't publish polished war stories after the fact. I document while the alert is still open — the false leads, the pivot that finally worked, the IOC that turned out to matter. That's the whole premise: **show the work, not just the win.**

```
> whoami
zein.al-ali — SOC analyst in training
> current_objective
turn raw alert queues into clean, defensible incident writeups
> philosophy
no theory without a triage ticket to back it up
```

<br/>

## ⟡ What I'm Doing Right Now

I'm actively working through **LetsDefend's** live monitoring queue — phishing attempts, brute-force patterns, suspicious PowerShell, network anomalies. Every alert I close gets logged with its context, my reasoning, and a MITRE ATT&CK mapping.

I'm not claiming finished case studies yet — I'm building the habit of writing them **as I go**, so the archive below fills in over time instead of arriving all at once.
